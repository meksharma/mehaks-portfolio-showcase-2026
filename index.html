Project 01 Microsoft, 2024-26

Reimagining How Users Summarize Emails on Outlook

Project summary

Summarize is Outlook Mobile's most-used Copilot scenario, but success was never just about generating summaries. The real challenge was helping users quickly understand complex conversations and take action without adding cognitive load.

With this redesign, the experience evolved from a reactive AI feature into an intelligent, embedded experience that lives within the reading flow. This helped Summarize evolve from a standalone AI feature into the future agentic experiences across Outlook.

With the introduction of the new Copilot Card framework, we successfully increased the Mobile MAU by 7.5% and Retention rates by 36.03%.

Market: Global  |  Platform: Mobile (iOS, Android) and iPad  |  Release: 2024-Present

My role

As the Lead Product Designer for Outlook Mobile's AI-powered email summaries, I was responsible for making summaries faster to understand, easier to discover, and more useful in the flow of reading email.

I led the mobile experience for bringing summaries directly onto the email canvas, defining how the summary is surfaced, generated and consumed, including card interactions, loading states, citations and contextual next actions. I also worked on the intelligence behind the experience, designing and iterating prompts, evaluating AI-generated outputs, and shaping shorter, more scannable responses for mobile.

My work also helped establish Summarize as an early implementation of Outlook's broader Copilot Card Framework, creating a pattern for bringing AI assistance directly into users' existing workflows.

Here are a few ways I brought that vision to life:

Experience evolution

01

Making AI summaries easier to consume

How might we turn long AI responses into something people can understand at a glance?

Early summaries were verbose and difficult to scan, particularly on mobile. I worked directly on the AI output as part of the UX, designing and testing prompts, reviewing generated responses, and finalised a format that prioritised shorter, more scannable summaries. I also defined the mobile-specific guidance for content and contextual actions, so the intelligence behind the experience worked with the interface rather than against it.

02

Bringing AI directly into the reading flow

How might we make AI useful without asking people to leave their email?

Summarise previously relied on people finding and triggering the feature. I helped design the on-canvas summary experience, bringing AI-generated summaries directly into Outlook's reading pane. My work covered how the card behaves from entry point → loading → summary → citations → next actions, while balancing the summary with the email content people came to read. This work became part of Outlook's broader Copilot Card Framework direction across mobile and desktop.

Project 02 Microsoft

Making Teams Search Faster & More Discoverable

Overview

Search was an underused capability on Teams Mobile, despite being available to 14.5M users, or 23% of Teams Mobile WAU. The opportunity was to make Search easier to understand and use, helping people discover that they could find more than just people, including messages, chats, meetings and files, while reducing abandonment.

My role

As the Product Designer for Teams Mobile Search, I was responsible for improving the search journey across iOS and Android, from helping users understand what they could search for to helping them formulate better queries.

I used customer research, behavioural data and experimentation to identify key drop-off points, then redesigned the Zero Input and Autosuggest experiences. The work shipped across Teams Mobile and delivered up to 7.77% lower abandonment, 1.27% higher Search Success Rate and 3.83% higher CCR.

Here are a few ways I brought that strategy to life:

Strategy in practice

01

Making Search useful before a query

Redesigned the empty search state to surface recently searched chats, files and more content types, helping users discover what Teams Search could find before typing.

02

Helping users formulate better searches

Redesigned Autosuggest to expose a broader range of searchable content while users typed, guiding them towards more relevant searches.

03

Creating cross-platform coherence

Brought Teams Mobile closer to the established desktop search model, creating a more predictable experience as users moved between devices.

Project 03 Agoda, 2019-22

Transforming Agoda VIP program, from Hidden Benefits → Growth Engine

Project summary

Agoda's VIP program faced low awareness, unclear benefits, and declining engagement. I helped redesign the experience across app and web, making loyalty benefits easier to discover, understand, and use throughout the booking journey.

Experience evolution

02

Benefits across the journey

Integrated VIP messaging into home, search, property, booking, and post-booking experiences.

03
