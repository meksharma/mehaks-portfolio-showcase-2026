Overview

Summarize is one of the strongest Copilot features inside Microsoft Outlook, with over 2.3 million users who have tried it on iOS alone. I joined as the first principles designer in August 2024 and owned the feature end-to-end - from introducing AI to Outlook mobile users for the first time, to integrating it into Copilot Chat, to currently prototyping the next evolution of how AI surfaces inside the inbox.

Lead designer across all phases of Summarize. Beyond design, I contributed to prompt engineering, ran experiments, and tracked daily usage to refine the AI output quality. In Phase 3, I experimented with vibe-coded design flows and prototyping - bringing the vision to life beyond just mockups.

How I work

01

Think in systems, not screens

The most important design decisions happen before anything goes into Figma. I push back on assumptions, reframe problems, and make sure the team is solving the right thing before we spend time solving it well.

02

Accountability doesn't end at handoff

I don't consider a feature done when the specs are delivered. I stay in the work through engineering, track usage after launch, and iterate until the experience actually performs the way it was designed to.

03

Make the vision tangible

Vibe coding is an integral part of my design toolkit. Showing stakeholders something real, connected to live data, moves alignment faster than any presentation deck. And in the world of AI, there's no better way to prove a direction works than to build it.
